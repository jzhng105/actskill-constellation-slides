# Privacy policy: Constellation Slides

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Constellation Slides is a Claude skill published by ZNSTARS. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## What the skill does with your data

- The skill's scripts run on your own computer or in your own Claude session. They read the model output folder and
  story file you point them at, and write the presentation files to the folder you choose.
- **ZNSTARS receives nothing.** The skill has no server, account, telemetry, analytics or tracking. It does not send
  your model results, files or usage to ZNSTARS or to anyone else.
- When Claude helps you use the skill, your conversation and files are handled by Anthropic under the terms and
  privacy policy of your Claude plan, not by ZNSTARS.

## Network requests made by the presentations

The interactive page the skill builds loads two things from the internet when someone opens it:

- the three.js graphics library from `cdnjs.cloudflare.com`, and
- the theme's fonts from Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`).

Those providers receive the viewer's IP address and browser details, as with any website that loads them. No model data
is sent to them. If you publish a presentation (for example as a claude.ai Artifact or on your own website), who can
see it is decided by you and by that host.
